FAQ

Delivery questions answered

How do I track my order?+
Go to our Track Order page and enter your Order ID. You'll see real-time status updates including cutting, stitching, quality check, and delivery stages.
Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?+
Yes — please contact us within 24 hours of placing your order via WhatsApp or phone. Once the garment is dispatched, changes may not be possible.
Do you deliver outside Bengaluru, Karnataka?+
Currently we deliver within Bengaluru, Karnataka. For orders outside Bengaluru region, please contact us — we may arrange courier delivery for an additional charge depending on location.
What if my garment doesn't fit correctly?+
We offer one free alteration on all orders if the measurements submitted by you matched our tailor's stitching. Visit our store or contact us to arrange a correction.
How is the garment packaged for delivery?+
Every garment is carefully folded, wrapped in tissue, placed in a garment bag and boxed securely. Plus plan orders include premium gift-style packaging.
When is payment collected?+
For home delivery, we accept Cash on Delivery, UPI, or bank transfer. Payment can also be arranged via WhatsApp before dispatch. Card payments are accepted at our Bengaluru store.
